# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 2026-03-27

This privacy policy applies to the **Sidestep** application ("the App") created by blankdotdev. The App is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

## Information Collection and Use

**We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.** 

The App is designed to operate locally on your device. Its primary function is to intercept social media URLs, remove tracking parameters locally, and redirect them to alternative, privacy-preserving frontends within your browser. 

- **No Tracking or Analytics:** The App has zero analytics, zero crash reporting frameworks, and zero tracking elements installed.
- **Local Processing:** All URL cleaning and processing happens entirely on your device. No data is sent to us or any third-party servers by the App.
- **Preview Fetching:** If you opt-in to fetch previews, they are securely fetched using a privacy-respecting provider (such as DuckDuckGo) without sharing any of your personal details or usage history with the App developer.

## Children’s Privacy

Because the App does not collect any personal data from its users, it does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we will be able to take necessary actions.

## Security

We value your trust in using our App. However, please be aware that no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security. Since the App processes data completely locally, the responsibility of device security lies primarily with the device operating system constraints.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. These changes are effective immediately after they are posted.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us by creating an issue in the [GitHub Repository](https://github.com/blankdotdev/sidestep/issues).
